# Using Meal Planner

All members share one household's recipes, plans and shopping lists. The live
production address is pending release verification; this guide also applies to
a configured local preview.

## Recipes and cooking

Open Recipes to search or add a recipe. Give it a name and list each ingredient,
quantity, unit and optional preparation note. Decimals and fractions such as
1/2 or 1 1/2 are supported. Leave a quantity blank when it is unspecified.
If a form reports an error, correct that input and save again.

Use Edit to update ingredients or other recipe details. Notes can capture
adjustments for next time. Log a cooking date, optional rating and note from
the recipe page to keep its history.

## Plan a week

Open Plans and select a date; the app opens the week beginning on its Monday.
Add recipes to days, or type custom meals such as leftovers or dinner out.
Multiple meals can share a day. Reload to see the saved plan. Week notes can
record schedule constraints; Log all meals records each recipe on its planned day.

Shopping uses each planned recipe once even when that recipe appears on several
days. Enter the quantities you intend to purchase in recipes or extras; automatic
serving scaling and copying a previous week are proposed future features.

## Purchasing rules and shopping

Use Pantry to choose how ingredients are bought:

- Every week: appears on every list. Its amount is the minimum needed, rather
  than an extra amount added to recipe demand.
- Pantry: appears in the check-the-pantry prompts when a recipe needs it.
- Per recipe: appears when the plan includes a recipe that uses it.

Open a plan's Shopping list. Compatible mass or volume units combine; count
units such as cans and cloves stay distinct. Move a pantry item onto the list
when you are low, and use have it to return it to the pantry prompts.

Check purchases off, skip items you already have, restore skipped items, or add
one-off extras. Changes persist after reload. Uncheck all resets checked
purchases. Recipe changes update the derived list.

Copy as text includes purchases and their checked status. Copy for Trello
includes remaining unchecked purchases. Copy meals for Trello exports the plan.
Print uses the list's print layout.

## Sharing and accounts

A week's share link lets anyone with that link view its plan and shopping list
without logging in. The shared page is read-only; give the link only to people
who should see that week's information.

Settings lets a signed-in member add another member or change their password.
Changing or resetting a password signs out all prior sessions; log in again
with the replacement password. Repeated failed logins can lock that email for
the rest of a fifteen-minute window. Sign out with the account control in the
navigation. Ask the named operator for an account reset if needed; self-service
account recovery is a proposed future feature.
